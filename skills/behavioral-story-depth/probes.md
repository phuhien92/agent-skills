# Probe bank

Follow-ups for **Drill** mode. Pick the one aimed at the weakest part of the last answer. Ask one at a time. Written in this repo's own words.

## Ownership of the action (use when the answer says "we")

- What was your part, specifically, versus the team's?
- If you had been out that week, what wouldn't have happened?
- Which decision in this story was yours to make?

## Judgment and alternatives

- What else did you consider? Why did it lose?
- What would have happened if you'd done the obvious thing?
- What information did you not have, and how did you decide anyway?
- Looking back, was it the right call? How do you know?

## Evidence and depth

- How did you find the root cause? Walk me through the steps.
- What data did you look at? What surprised you in it?
- Where did the metric and the anecdotes disagree?
- How did you verify the fix actually worked?

## Friction and influence

- Who disagreed? What was their concern?
- What were they protecting that you hadn't considered?
- How did you get them to yes? What did you give up?
- After the decision, how did you work with the person who lost the argument?

## Stakes and results

- What would have happened if nobody acted?
- How did you measure success? Who else agreed it was a success?
- What's the number, and how was it measured?
- What happened after you left the project?

## Self-critique

- What would you do differently?
- What feedback did you get on this? What did you change because of it?
- What part of this are you least proud of?

## Scale and leverage (seniority probes)

- Did this change how the team works, or just this one outcome?
- Who else used what you built?
- What did you set up so it wouldn't depend on you?

## Slip checklist (what to call out)

| Slip | Sounds like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "We" drift | "We decided to…" | "I proposed…, the team agreed" |
| Vague result | "It improved a lot" | One number or one concrete outcome |
| New number | A metric not in the built story | Use the recorded one, or "I don't have the exact number" |
| Missing decision | Lists tasks, no choice | Name the fork and why |
| Self-praise | "This shows my ownership" | Cut it; say the fact |
| Ramble | Past 2 minutes on the opener | Stop at the result; let them probe |
