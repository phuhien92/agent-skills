---
description: Draw or redesign characters, mascots, faces, icons or cards in a project's own style, as SVG, showing distinct options before polishing one. Use when the user asks for a mockup, a new character or set, a redesign or an "ultimate card", or says the art looks ugly, generic or too similar.
---

Follow the `house-style-design` skill.

Read the house style and its rules before drawing. Mockup first: for a new character or set, show three distinct directions before polishing one. After a vague "ugly" or "generic", ask which part failed before replacing the concept. Keep characters original. Render, check at phone size (sets also in greyscale), and say which parts are placeholders. Don't wire designs into the project unasked.
